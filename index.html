02 / 04 Boona Cue 2026 · Beta, in development Text → animation · any engine

Words in. The exact performance out.

  • 0Blinks asked for, blinks delivered
  • 0Nods asked for, nods delivered
  • 0Cues dropped or invented

Boona Cue turns words from any AI into animation for AI characters. Tell it "blink twice, then nod three times" and the character blinks exactly twice and nods exactly three times, on any engine.

The language model decides what to say. Cue makes sure the body keeps its word: the exact performance, every time, nothing extra. It is in beta and not yet released.

02 ApproachHow a project runs
  1. 01

    Listen first

    What does the business actually do all day? I start there, not with the AI wish list.

  2. 02

    Map the hours

    Where the time goes, written down. We pick the one flow worth automating first and the number we expect it to move.

  3. 03

    Prototype on real data

    A working slice within days, on your data, not a slide. If it does not earn its keep, we stop early and cheap.

  4. 04

    Instrument before shippingRule

    Telemetry first. I once ran production without it and debugged the outage by feel. Never again: if we cannot see it, we do not ship it.

  5. 05

    Dates come as a rangeRule

    Targets I once set to a single day got missed. Now every date is a range, and the range holds.

  6. 06

    Hand over, properly

    Documentation, dashboards, and a team that can run it without me. I am the architect, not the dependency.
